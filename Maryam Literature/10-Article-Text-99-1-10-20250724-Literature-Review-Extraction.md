## Paper: Prosody Analysis to Recognize Speech Disorders among the Elderly

---

### 1. Full Title of the Paper:
Prosody Analysis to Recognize Speech Disorders among the Elderly

### 2. Author(s) and Year of Publication:
Cecep Agus, Nur Alifah (2025)

### 3. Source / Publisher:
Journal of Psycholinguistics, Volume 2 No 1, January-June 2025, Pages 125-137. Available online at http://jpl.staiku.ac.id

### 4. IEEE Reference Format:
C. Agus and N. Alifah, "Prosody analysis to recognize speech disorders among the elderly," *Journal of Psycholinguistics*, vol. 2, no. 1, pp. 125-137, Jan.-Jun. 2025.

### 5. Research Domain / Area:
Speech Disorders Detection, Prosodic Analysis, Geriatric Healthcare, Speech Therapy, Early Detection Systems

### 6. Aim / Objective of the Study:
The study aims to develop a prosody analysis model that can be applied to identify speech disorders in the elderly. The objective is to recognize speech impairments through prosodic analysis, enabling early screening and more targeted clinical and speech therapy interventions to maintain the quality of life for the elderly.

### 7. Methodology Used:
Qualitative research methodology involving in vivo voice recordings, interviews, and signal analysis using sound processing software. The method includes measuring prosodic parameters such as frequency, pause duration, and voice stress patterns in real communication contexts.

### 8. Technologies / Tools Mentioned (if any):
Sound processing software, prosodic analysis tools, signal processing techniques for measuring pitch frequency, speech rhythm, and voice intensity

### 9. Key Findings / Contributions:
The research found a decrease in pitch frequency range, slowness of speech rhythm, and decreased voice intensity, indicating a decline in phonation function among the elderly. The prosodic approach can serve as an early screening tool, enabling more targeted clinical and speech therapy interventions. The study demonstrates that prosodic aspects can be early indicators of declining language function, even before physical symptoms become apparent.

### 10. Identified Limitations (if stated):
Not explicitly stated in the abstract. However, the paper acknowledges challenges such as variations in education levels, health conditions, and native language background among elderly participants.

### 11. Relevance to the Current Project:

The study demonstrates the application of prosodic features (pitch frequency, speech rhythm, voice intensity) for analyzing speech characteristics, which directly supports tone analysis in interview contexts. The early detection approach through prosodic analysis aligns with the need for real-time tone assessment in interview practice platforms.
